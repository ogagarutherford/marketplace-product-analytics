# Business Questions — Kroma Marketplace

Kroma is a fictional DACH home & lifestyle marketplace connecting ~320 independent sellers with
shoppers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland (synthetic data, July–December 2025).

The Product team asks:

1. **Funnel:** Where do shoppers drop off between landing and purchase, and how does this differ by device, channel and new vs returning visitors?
2. **Acquisition:** Which channels bring visitors who convert and come back, and what does a paid-channel customer cost?
3. **Retention:** Do first-time buyers return? How do monthly cohorts retain, and which segments (RFM) deserve retention spend?
4. **Experiment:** In November we tested a simplified checkout. Did it increase purchase conversion and revenue per user, without harming guardrails? Should Product ship it, and for whom?
5. **Drivers:** Controlling for device, channel and visitor type, what is associated with conversion? (Regression — association, not causation.)

The final deliverable is a recommendation that starts: *"Based on the experiment and behavioural evidence, Product should…"*
